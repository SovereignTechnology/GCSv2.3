Our People

Meet the Team

José Max Hernández Sandoval

CEO · Engineer — Director de Obra

GCS's CEO and a licensed engineer, Max holds the COAMSS/OPAMSS Director de Obra accreditation — the highest credential a civil engineer or architect can earn, requiring nine years of government-verified experience. It authorizes him to take legal responsibility for construction of any kind. Today no project can be built — and no company can legally call itself a construction firm — without a Director de Obra.
